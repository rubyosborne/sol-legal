The short version

This summary is here to be read. The full policy below is the complete picture.

  • Red Wellness is a training and wellness coach for iPhone, provided by Resonance Labs Limited, a New Zealand company run by one person, Ruby Osborne.
  • Red Wellness collects what you tell it, what you log, and the Apple Health data you allow it to read.
  • Your Red Wellness account data is stored with Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Sydney, Australia.
  • Emails you send us (to support@, hello@ or privacy@) are forwarded by Cloudflare and stored in Google's Gmail, which keeps mail in the United States and other countries.
  • To write your plans, briefs, monthly reports and chat replies, Red Wellness sends the information needed to Anthropic, a US company, whose Claude AI models do the writing. Anthropic stores it in the United States and may process it in other countries. This includes health information, such as anything you tell Red Wellness about injuries, conditions or medications, a rehab or physio programme you add, things you choose to log such as alcohol, medication taken or the supplements you take, and the text of lab results you add. Anthropic does not train its models on that information and deletes it within 30 days, with limited exceptions explained in section 5.
  • Red keeps a short list of things you've told it so it can coach you better. You can see and delete it in Settings, What Red remembers.
  • If you choose things to log, such as sauna or eating late, Red Wellness looks for patterns between them and your own sleep, recovery and training, only ever in your own data. Friends never see them.
  • If you use tapping or meditation, Red Wellness keeps your sessions, including what you type in them, until you delete them. What you type in a session never goes to the AI or into our server logs. The topics you give a session go to Anthropic only if you choose that.
  • If you add friends or train together, other Red Wellness users see what section 6 describes, and nothing more. In a training-together goal this includes members who are not your friends.
  • Nobody at Red Wellness looks at your data as a matter of course. The exceptions are replies you report, replies the app's automatic checks flag, and the questions the app answers on your phone without the AI, which Ruby reads to find and fix mistakes in Red's answers. They are kept 90 days (section 5.5).
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no data brokers. Your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and its data inside the app at any time (Settings, Delete account). You can download a copy of your data in the app at any time (Settings, Download my data), and ask for anything else, such as a correction, by emailing privacy@resonancelabs.co.nz.

2. What we collect

We collect information from three places: from you directly, from Apple Health with your permission, and from Apple when you sign in. We do not buy information about you or collect it from anywhere else, except where another Red Wellness user does something that involves you (section 2.6).

2.1 Account information

  • Sign in with Apple identifier. Apple gives us a stable identifier for your Apple ID. We store it so we can recognise you next time.
  • Email address. Sign in with Apple may give us your email address, or a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". We use it only at the moment of sign-in and do not store it.
  • Your display name. The name you give Red Wellness during setup. Friends and training partners see this name.
  • Your friend code. A random code (for example RED-ABC123) that you can share so others can add you.
  • Session tokens. When you sign in on a device, we issue a token so the app stays signed in. We store only a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of it.
  • Sign in with Apple token. When you sign in, Apple gives the app a one-time code. Our server can swap it with Apple for a token that lets us end Red Wellness's Sign in with Apple access when you delete your account (section 8). When we hold one, we store the newest one on its own, use it for nothing else, never write it to our logs, and delete it with your account.
  • App check key. The app makes a key on your phone that Apple certifies as belonging to a genuine copy of Red Wellness (Apple App Attest). We store the public half of that key, a counter, and which Red Wellness account last used it, so our server can tell real app requests from imitations. It says nothing about you or your health.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a token that lets our server send notifications to that phone through Apple's push service (section 5.1). We store it with your account, together with a random identifier for that copy of the app, whether it is a test or App Store build, your Morning readiness and Morning plan notification switches, a one-way scrambled (hashed) copy of that phone's session token, and when it was last updated. We use it only to send your morning notifications, and never write it to our logs. It says nothing about your health.
  • Your AI permission. A record of the permission you gave for AI processing: when, which wording you saw and which app version. We keep the history of any changes and delete it with your account.
  • Time zone and app settings, such as which areas of Red Wellness you have switched on and how you like notifications.
  • There is no Red Wellness password. We never see, hold or store one.

2.2 What you tell Red Wellness during setup

  • your name or what you would like to be called;
  • why you came to Red Wellness, your goals, any target event and its date;
  • your current training, how often you move, the kinds of training you do, and the days, times, places and session lengths that suit you;
  • how your energy and sleep have been;
  • how you eat and whether you track food, and any food constraints such as allergies or a diet you follow;
  • optional any current injuries you choose to tell Red Wellness about: which body areas, how the injury affects your training, and anything else you write about it;
  • optional any rehab or physio programme you want built into your training, and when it ends or is next reviewed (for example a physio review date). You can type or paste it, pick a PDF or photo, or scan a paper sheet with your camera; the text is read on your phone, identifying details such as your name and contact details are removed, and only the text is uploaded. If you ask Red Wellness to suggest some work for an injury instead, Red Wellness records that you asked;
  • optional any health conditions or medications you choose to mention (a free-text question);
  • optional your birth year and height;
  • optional your sex (female, male, or prefer not to say). Red Wellness uses it to calculate your training load from your heart rate and, if you ask for suggested nutrition targets, your resting energy. If you skip it, or choose prefer not to say, Red Wellness uses a value halfway between the female and male calculations;
  • optional if you track fuel, daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat targets, if you set them, and where they came from (Red Wellness's suggestion, typed by you, or from your nutritionist). Red Wellness suggests them from your birth year, height, sex, weight, recent workouts (or, without watch workouts, how often you said you move) and, if you added one, a body-composition scan's resting energy, and from two answers on the same step: how active your normal day is outside your workouts (mostly sitting, on your feet some of the day, on your feet most of the day, or physical work) and what you want your eating to support (keeping your weight steady, building muscle, or losing weight gradually; weight loss is not offered if your height and weight put you under a BMI of 18.5, or before your height and weight are known). Both answers are stored with your targets, with the date you set them, so the screen can show them next time, and they become part of your goals, which Red reads when it writes your plans, briefs and replies (section 5.1). You can skip this step and set or change them later in Goals and Training;
  • which watch you wear, if any. Red Wellness keeps this on your phone only and uses it to read your watch's numbers correctly;
  • opt-in only whether you want Red Wellness to track your menstrual cycle, and, if you do and Apple Health has no record, the date of your last period. If you answer "No, or not applicable", your phone keeps that answer and sends our server a note that you have no cycle, so your wrist temperature can be used in your patterns (section 2.4). Because it is about your reproductive health, we treat it as sensitive. If you later turn cycle tracking on, your phone tells our server the note no longer applies. If you answer "Yes, but don't track" or "Rather not say", that answer is not sent.

Red Wellness stores your injury, rehab and condition answers, keeps a list of your injuries and rehab with whether each is active or resolved (you can change it in Goals and Training), and sends them to Anthropic word for word so the AI can take them into account (section 5.1).

Every body and health question can be skipped. Skipping means Red Wellness has less to go on, not that it stops working.

2.3 Apple Health data

Red Wellness asks Apple Health for permission to read the kinds of data below. iOS shows you each one and you choose which to allow. Red Wellness only asks for a kind of data if the area of Red Wellness that uses it is switched on, and you can switch areas off in Settings. If you deny a kind of data, Red Wellness does not receive it.

In plain terms, Red Wellness can read:

  • Workouts: what kind of workout, how long, how far, the energy you burned and your average and highest heart rate during it.
  • Sleep: how long you slept each night, how much of it was deep and REM sleep, and the time each night's main sleep started.
  • Heart, breathing and recovery: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, walking heart rate, breathing rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature while you sleep, and VO2 max (an estimate of your fitness).
  • Body measurements: weight, body fat percentage and lean body mass.
  • Daily activity: steps, active energy and exercise minutes.
  • Nutrition logged in Apple Health: energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fibre and sugar, some vitamins and minerals, and caffeine.
  • opt-in only Menstrual cycle, only if you turn on cycle tracking: period flow and spotting.

Appendix A lists every individual Apple Health data type Red Wellness can read, and which area of Red Wellness uses it.

Red Wellness reads daily totals or averages, plus one record per workout, not every individual reading. When you first connect, Red Wellness reads up to one year of history (the last 365 days) so it can learn your baselines; after that it reads recent days.

Background sync. Red Wellness may sync new Health data to Red Wellness's server in the background, for example after a workout is saved, without the app being open. It goes only to Red Wellness's server.

Signs you may be getting sick. If you track readiness and your watch measures wrist temperature, Red Wellness checks each morning, on your phone, whether last night's wrist temperature was well above your own usual (after allowing for your cycle, if you track it) and your breathing rate, resting heart rate or heart rate variability also moved the same night, or your temperature was up two nights running. This is an inference about your health from your temperature, breathing rate, heart rate and heart rate variability. When it sees that, the app shows a heads-up and asks "Feeling unwell?", and sends our server a note that it did and which signals were up (not the readings), so your morning brief can suggest an easier day. Your answer is kept as "Felt unwell", one of the things you log. Red Wellness is not a medical device: the heads-up is a wellness suggestion, it can be wrong, it never diagnoses anything, and it never changes your plan by itself. If you are worried about how you feel, talk to a doctor.

Your cycle from your temperature. If you track your cycle and your watch measures wrist temperature, Red Wellness looks back over each cycle, on your phone, for the rise in your sleeping temperature that follows ovulation. From it, it estimates roughly when your ovulatory phase was in each past cycle, how much your temperature rises and how long your luteal phase lasts for you, and when your coming phases and your next period are likely. It uses these to place your cycle phases, to read your temperature in your readiness score, and to help plan your training and recovery. If your temperature comes back down and no period is logged, the app asks whether your period has started; nothing is logged unless you say so. This is an inference about your reproductive health, which we treat as sensitive. Your phone sends our server the dates of each rise it found, your own rise and luteal length, and whether it asked about a period, so your morning brief and your patterns use the same estimates (the nightly readings themselves arrive with your other Apple Health data). These are estimates for wellness, training and recovery only, and they can be wrong. Red Wellness is not a medical device, it is not a method of contraception or a way to plan a pregnancy, and it never tells you on which days you could or could not get pregnant. If you turn cycle tracking off, your phone stops working these out and stops sending them.

What Red Wellness writes to Apple Health. If you allow it, Red Wellness writes what you log in the app into Apple Health on your phone: the food you log (energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, and the fibre, sugar, vitamins and minerals Red Wellness knows for it), your meditation and tapping time as Mindful Minutes, a weight or height you type in, period or spotting entries (flow level and cycle start, or spotting), and a workout you logged by telling Red about it or on a workout card (its type, start and length, written about half an hour after it ends, and not written if your watch recorded the same session). These become part of your Apple Health record. Red Wellness never writes back anything it read from Apple Health or a device, and writes nothing else to Apple Health. You can stop it in Red Wellness's Settings (Save to Apple Health) or for each kind of data in iOS Settings, and deleting a tapping session in Red Wellness also deletes the Mindful Minutes it wrote for it.

Cycle tracking is off unless you turn it on. If you say no to cycle tracking or to food tracking during setup, Red Wellness does not ask for those Apple Health permissions. Apple Health data is used only to provide Red Wellness's health and fitness features, and never for advertising or marketing (section 3).

2.4 What you create while using Red Wellness

  • Chat. Your messages to Red and Red's replies, including the recent conversation and a summary of your current numbers that the app attaches so Red can answer in context.
  • Food logs. What you type or say about a meal, the barcode numbers of products you scan, and the foods, amounts and nutrients you confirm.
  • Foods and recipes you save, such as a product typed or photographed from its label, a meal you saved (including a meal saved as a recipe), or a recipe: the names and nutrition you enter, a recipe's ingredients, servings and cooked weight, a barcode number if you add one, and which foods you mark as favourites. They are only visible to you, they are kept until you delete them or your account, and they are included in Download my data.
  • Training logs and check-ins, such as sessions, loads, lifts, how a session went, a pain score and where it hurts. There is no longer a daily "took my supplements" check-in; supplements you add to the things you choose to log are kept by name, below (Red Wellness does not record doses). A supplements yes from before the check-in was removed stays in your history and in Download my data.
  • Notes and goal requests you send to Red Wellness in your own words.
  • Training plans you paste or upload, and what you tell Red Wellness when it interviews you about a plan (including baseline paces and longest run).
  • Injuries and rehab you add or change after setup (in Goals and Training), including a rehab or physio programme and its end point, and your answers when Red Wellness asks whether to keep or drop a rehab item. A programme read from a PDF, photo or camera scan is handled as in section 2.2: read on your phone, identifying details removed, only the text uploaded. A programme can name your physio or clinic; please remove anything you would rather not share before you save it.
  • Documents you add, such as lab results (for example blood tests) or body-composition scan reports. You pick a PDF or a photo, and the app reads the text from it on your phone. Before anything leaves your phone, Red Wellness removes identifying details from the text it read, such as your name, date of birth, NHI number, address, phone numbers, email addresses and the requesting doctor or clinic. It then uploads to our server the values you confirmed and the rest of the text, which contains the test names, results, units, reference ranges and test dates. Removal is automatic and may occasionally miss a detail printed in an unusual layout. The PDF or photo itself is not uploaded. For your monthly report, Red Wellness sends this text from your two most recent documents of each kind to Anthropic (section 5.1).
  • Weight and period entries you type into Red Wellness.
  • Things you choose to log. If you pick things to log, such as alcohol, acupuncture or having someone in your bed, Red Wellness keeps your list, your check-in time, and each yes or no with the day it was about and how you logged it (the evening check-in, the + on Home, or telling Red in chat). It also keeps your answer to "How was today?" (1 to 5) if you give one. Some are suggested from data the app already has, such as a big meal from your food log or a late workout from your Watch, and you can change those before saving. Eating late is never worked out for you: it is only what you log. Once a week, Red Wellness's server compares them with your own sleep, heart, training, food, pain and cycle data, your scores and your "How was today?" ratings, to find patterns in your own history; it also compares parts of that data with each other, such as a short night with how you slept the night after. That is done by our software, not by the AI, and only ever within your own data, never across people. The patterns it finds are stored with your account, together with a day-by-day summary of the numbers it used (kept for about a year), and shown in your monthly report and in Settings, Patterns, where you can hide any of them. A pattern starts as an early sign and becomes confirmed only if newer days show the same thing. Red may bring up a confirmed pattern in your morning brief or in chat on a day your own numbers are the kind it describes, at most once a day and three times a week; it never brings up an early sign or a pattern you hid, and you can stop it in Settings, Patterns ("Red can mention patterns"). Your list can include the supplements you take, each logged as a yes or no under the name you give it (for example "Magnesium"); Red Wellness does not record doses. Supplements are treated as sensitive, so their names never appear in a notification or the home-screen widget. Friends never see your list, your answers or the patterns.
  • Wrist temperature in your patterns. When Red Wellness's server looks for patterns in your own data (above), it includes your nightly wrist temperature only where your cycle cannot hide the result: if you have not logged a cycle and told us you are male, or told us at setup that you have no cycle ("No, or not applicable", section 2.2), it uses your temperature as measured; if you track your cycle and your phone has measured your own temperature rise in at least two cycles, it uses each night's temperature with that rise taken off the nights after ovulation. Otherwise your temperature is left out of your patterns.
  • Tapping and meditation sessions. If you use Mindfulness, Red Wellness keeps each tapping session: when it started and ended, your setup statement and how you said it again afterwards, how strong the feeling was before and after (0 to 10), whether it was about a specific event, the parts of the body you picked and any words you added about where you felt it, your notes, which helper statement was shown, and the topics you gave it. It keeps your list of topics, including any you rename or merge. For meditation it keeps when you meditated, for how long, the length you picked and any note. Sessions are kept on our server, so they move with you to a new phone, and on your phone in a file iOS keeps encrypted while the phone is locked. Everything you type in a session (your statements, notes, the words about where you felt it and a meditation note) never goes to the AI and is never written to our server logs. Your topic names go to Anthropic only if you answered yes to "Should Red see what your sessions are about?"; you can change that any time in Settings ("Red can see my tapping topics"). Saving a session also logs tapping or meditation for that day as one of the things you choose to log. Sessions and topics are kept until you delete them or your account, are included in Download my data, and are never shown to friends.
  • Replies you report. If you press and hold one of Red's replies, the title of one of its cards, or a morning brief, and choose Report issue, Red Wellness sends us that reply (for a card, which card it was), your message just before it, what you typed about what was wrong, when each was sent, the app version, a reference to the request, and how the reply was produced: written by the AI, or by the app on your phone (and, if the app reworded your question to answer it, the reworded question). Nothing else from your data goes with it. Ruby reads it to find and fix what went wrong (section 5.5).
  • Workouts you tell Red about. When you describe a workout you did or are doing in chat, or log one in the app, Red shows a card to confirm it. Only if you tap Log it, Red Wellness keeps it in your training history like a watch workout: the type of workout, when it started, how long it was, the distance if you gave one, and a short description of what you did (such as your exercises and sets), on your phone and on our server. It counts toward your training load and is read by the AI with the rest of your training history (section 5.1). If your Apple Watch recorded the same session, the watch's workout is kept instead and your description is kept as a note.
  • Exercises you ask us to add. If you swap in or add an exercise that Red Wellness's exercise library does not know and choose "Ask for this to be added to the library", Red Wellness sends us the exercise name, your note if you wrote one, when you sent it and the app version. Nothing else from your data or your session goes with it (section 5.5).
  • Questions the app answers on your phone. Some questions, such as "how did I sleep?", are answered by the app itself on your phone, without the AI. For each one, Red Wellness sends us your question, the question the app treated it as (for example "what makes up my sleep?"), what kind of answer it gave (words, a card or both, and which card), the app version and the time. It does not send the answer itself unless a check below flags it. This is so Ruby can see when the app has misunderstood a question (section 5.5).
  • Replies the app flags. Red Wellness checks every reply automatically on your phone, whether the AI or the app wrote it, for three signs that something went wrong: a reply that is only a card with no words, an empty reply, and you asking the same thing again in similar words within about a minute. When a check flags a reply, Red Wellness sends us your question, the reply, your next question if that is what flagged it, which check flagged it, the app version, the time, and how the reply was produced (with a reference to the request, or the question the app treated yours as). Nothing else from your data goes with it.

2.5 What Red Wellness produces about you

Red Wellness produces information from the above: readiness, effort and fuel scores (calculated on your phone; once a day the app also sends our server the previous day's readiness, effort and fuel scores, so patterns in your own data can include them; section 6.1 says when a friend can see one), daily briefs, weekly and longer-term training plans, monthly reports, progress toward your goals, and a record of the coaching decisions Red Wellness has made for you. We treat all of this as your personal information too.

What Red remembers. Red Wellness keeps a short list of things you've told it so it can coach you better, such as when you like to train or that you walk the dog in the mornings. It holds up to 30 short items, each with the date it was saved. Red picks them from your chat messages and tidies the list once a week. Red is instructed not to save passing comments or anything the app records elsewhere, including pain, injury, illness, medical conditions, medication, goals, training and food. That is an instruction to the AI, not a guarantee, so you can see every item it saves: when Red saves one, a line under its reply says "Red will remember: ...", and tapping it opens What Red remembers. The list is sent to Anthropic with each plan, brief, monthly report and chat reply (section 5.1). You can see and delete any item, or the whole list, in Settings, What Red remembers.

2.6 Information about you from other Red Wellness users

If another Red Wellness user adds you as a friend, invites you to train together, mutes, blocks or reports you, we hold a record of that. A report includes the reason the other person typed. Red Wellness does not tell you when someone mutes, blocks or reports you, to protect the other person. We tell you about this collection here, and the app shows you friend requests and training-together requests when they arrive.

2.7 Voice

If you use voice input, Red Wellness uses your microphone only from when you tap the microphone button until you tap it again. Your speech is turned into text on your phone by a speech recognition model that is built into the app. The audio is held in your phone's memory only while it is being turned into text. It is never saved and never leaves your phone. Only the text, after you have seen it and chosen to send it (for example "two eggs and porridge"), is sent to Red Wellness, the same as if you had typed it.

2.8 What Red Wellness does not collect

  • No location, contacts, or photo library access. (When you add a document, you pick the single file yourself.)
  • No camera images. Red Wellness uses your camera only to scan a food barcode, to photograph a food's nutrition label, or to scan a paper rehab or physio programme, and only while you are doing it. The image is read on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere. Only the barcode number, the nutrition values you check and save, or the programme text you check and save (with identifying details removed) go to Red Wellness's server.
  • No advertising identifier, device fingerprinting or cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools from other companies.
  • No payment details. Red Wellness is free. If we add paid features, purchases will go through Apple, and Apple does not give us your card details.

3. Why we use it

We use your information to provide Red Wellness to you, and for nothing unrelated. Specifically:

  1. to build and adjust your training plan, daily brief and monthly report;
  2. to answer you in chat with your own history in mind;
  3. to estimate your food intake from what you log;
  4. to calculate and show your scores, trends and progress;
  5. to show the friends and training partners you have chosen what you have chosen to share with them (section 6);
  6. to send you notifications on your phone;
  7. to keep Red Wellness secure, prevent abuse (for example daily usage limits), and investigate reports between users;
  8. to find and fix problems in Red Wellness, including wrong or missing answers from Red;
  9. to meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your information, and in particular your Apple Health data, for advertising, marketing, selling, data mining or profiling for anyone else. We do not use it to train AI models, and we do not let our providers do so.

Automated output. Red Wellness's plans, briefs and replies are produced automatically by AI from your data. They are suggestions for your training and wellbeing, and they can be wrong; the app says so when you sign up, in Settings, and at the end of each day's morning brief, which always closes with "Red, your AI wellness coach". Red Wellness does not make decisions that have legal effects on you or that decide access to any service, credit, insurance or employment.

5. Who we share it with

6. Friends and training together

These features are optional. Nothing is shared with anyone until you take an action described here.

6.1 Friends

You add a friend by exchanging friend codes; there is no public search. Once you are friends, each of you sees the other's display name. For each friend separately you choose what they can see, using three switches that all start off:

  • My training: the title of each day's session in your current week, such as "Run ~8 km" or "Rest", and the name of your training goal.
  • My readiness: your readiness score, a number from 0 to 100, and the day it is from.
  • My fuel: your fuel score, a number from 0 to 100, and the day it is from.

A friend never sees Red Wellness's written summaries, the notes in your plan, the health numbers behind your scores, your chat, notes, check-ins, documents or setup answers, or anything outside the switches you turn on. Your readiness and fuel scores are worked out on your phone. A friend sees one only while you share it with them: for that, your phone sends the current score, and we keep the latest one and delete it when no friend can see it any more. The daily history of your scores that your phone sends for your own patterns (section 2.5) is never shown to friends. You can change a switch at any time and it takes effect straight away. Removing or blocking a friend ends all sharing both ways.

6.2 Training together

You can ask to train toward a friend's training goal (only if that friend shares their training with you), and they can accept. Joining replaces your own training goal with the shared one. Asking to join, or accepting someone's request, is your permission for the sharing below.

Everyone in the goal, including people who are not your friends, can see:

  • your display name;
  • your progress row: how you are doing against your own plan this week, and how far you are toward your own target, both as percentages;
  • notifications about your significant sessions, sent to every member whatever your friend sharing switches say:
    • your hardest session of the week (for a run, this can include distance and pace, for example "a 10 km run at 5:30 pace");
    • a personal best, which can include a lift's weight and reps, a run's distance and pace, or a VO2 max value;
    • a race or test day;
    • reaching 25, 50, 75 or 100 percent of the target, with the goal's name.

You see the same about the other members.

If you choose to follow the goal owner's weekly pattern, Red Wellness uses only the kinds of sessions on each of the owner's days, never their numbers.

Everyone's plan is built from their own data, and nobody's numbers are copied into anyone else's account. You can leave at any time, the owner can end the goal, and blocking or removing someone takes them out of any goal you share. You can mute one person's notifications without leaving.

6.3 Blocking and reporting

You can block anyone. You can report a user with the report button and add a reason. Each report is saved in Red Wellness's database and we are alerted that one was filed. Ruby reads it in the database. We aim to review reports within 24 hours, and always within 2 working days, and she decides what to do, which can include suspending or closing the reported account. The person you report is not told who reported them.

While an account is suspended, its owner is signed out and cannot sign back in, and other users no longer see it in their friends lists or shared goals. Its data is kept, not deleted, and its owner can still download a copy and delete the account from the sign-in screen. We record when the suspension started and ended, and Ruby may keep a short private note about why. If the suspension is lifted, everything is restored as it was.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have a Red Wellness account, because Red Wellness's coaching depends on your history: your plan next week is built from the weeks before it. Older health records are moved to archives but are still kept, so Red Wellness can compare seasons and years.

  • Account, setup answers, health history, logs, plans, reports, documents, injuries and rehab: until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • What Red remembers: until you delete an item or the whole list in Settings, What Red remembers, or Red Wellness's weekly tidy removes it (merged with a similar item, or its time has passed). Removed when you delete your account.
  • Tapping and meditation sessions and topics: until you delete a session in the app, or your account.
  • Chat history: 7 days, on our servers and on your phone. Our database deletes expired messages automatically, which can take a few more days.
  • Session tokens: until you sign out on that device or delete your account.
  • Sign in with Apple token: until you delete your account. Each sign-in replaces it with a new one.
  • App check key: until you delete your account, or 180 days after the app last used it.
  • Push notification token: until you delete your account, or until Apple tells us it no longer works (for example after you delete the app). After you sign out on a phone, our server stops sending to it and deletes its token the next time it sends your morning notifications.
  • Server logs: 7 days.
  • Reports you file about another user: kept until you delete your account, and deleted with it. Also kept after the reported account is deleted, when the report no longer leads to that person's name, Apple ID or data.
  • Replies you report: 90 days, then our database deletes the report automatically (which can take a few more days). Deleted sooner if you delete your account. We delete the email copy from our mailbox within 90 days.
  • Exercises you ask us to add to the library: 90 days, then our database deletes the request automatically (which can take a few more days). Deleted sooner if you delete your account. We delete the email copy from our mailbox within 90 days.
  • Questions the app answers on your phone, and replies the app flags: 90 days, then our database deletes them automatically (which can take a few more days). Deleted sooner if you delete your account. We delete the email copy of a flagged reply from our mailbox within 90 days.
  • Record that a missed-run notice was sent: 30 days, then our database deletes it automatically (which can take a few more days). Deleted sooner if you delete your account. We delete the email copy of a missed-run notice from our mailbox within 90 days.
  • Product details from Open Food Facts: kept in a shared cache on Red Wellness's server with no account attached: 30 days if the product was found, 7 days if it was not, and then deleted automatically.
  • Backups: we do not keep database backups. If we add them, we will update this policy and say how long they are kept.
  • At Anthropic: up to 30 days, or longer for flagged content (section 5.1).
  • Copies on your phone: until you delete the app or the account (section 8).

You can ask us to delete a specific item, such as a document, your cycle history or a conversation, without closing your account. If you ask us to delete information we are legally required to keep, we will tell you what and why, and delete the rest.

10. How we protect it

  • Sign in with Apple only, so there is no Red Wellness password to be guessed, reused or stolen.
  • Session tokens are stored only in hashed form, and the token on your phone is kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Requests that cost money or sign you in carry a signature from an Apple-certified key on your phone, so a script pretending to be Red Wellness can be told apart from the app.
  • All traffic between the app, our server and our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Data in our database is encrypted at rest by AWS.
  • Your tapping and meditation sessions are also kept on your phone in a file iOS keeps encrypted and unreadable while the phone is locked.
  • Access to the server and database is limited to Ruby and protected by multi-factor authentication on the AWS account's sign-in; programmatic access keys are kept only on Ruby's own computer.
  • We keep the number of companies that handle your data to four (AWS, Anthropic, Google and Cloudflare), plus Apple for sign-in.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that nothing will ever go wrong. Red Wellness is small and run by one person, and we have kept it simple on purpose. Section 11 says what we will do if something does go wrong.

11. If there is a privacy breach

If a privacy breach happens that it is reasonable to believe has caused or is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and you as soon as practicable, as the Privacy Act 2020 requires. Specifically:

  • we will not wait for certainty to warn you if waiting would leave you exposed.
