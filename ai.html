How Red uses AI

Last updated: 2026-11-03

The short version

  • The app's AI coach, Red, writes your plans and replies using Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, a company in the United States.
  • To write them, Red Wellness sends Anthropic what each job needs: your answers, logs, chats and recent Apple Health data. Anthropic is in the US and sometimes processes it in other countries.
  • Anthropic doesn't train its AI on your data and normally deletes it within 30 days (longer if its safety checks flag it).
  • US privacy law may not protect your data the way New Zealand's does.
  • Your account and everything Red Wellness writes for you is stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • You can withdraw anytime by turning AI off in Settings. Red Wellness can't work without AI, so that deletes your Red Wellness account.

What Red Wellness uses AI for

Red's daily briefs, weekly plans, monthly reports, chat replies and food estimates are all written by Claude. That's what makes Red a coach rather than a tracker: it reads your own numbers and writes advice for you.

Because it is written by AI, it can be wrong. Red Wellness tells you so when you sign up and in Settings, and every morning brief ends with "Red, your AI wellness coach".

Where your data goes

Here is exactly what happens when Red Wellness writes something for you.

1. Your phone sends your data to Red Wellness's server in Sydney

Red Wellness's server and database run on Amazon Web Services in Sydney, Australia. That's where your account, answers, logs and Apple Health numbers are stored.

2. Red Wellness's server sends Anthropic what that job needs

Anthropic, in the United States, receives the request, and Claude writes the plan, brief or reply.

3. Claude's reply comes back and is stored in Sydney

Your plan, brief or reply is saved in your Red Wellness account in Sydney. Anthropic keeps its copy only as section "What Anthropic does with it" describes.

What's sent, and what isn't

Depending on the job, Red Wellness can send Anthropic:

  • your name and setup answers, including anything you wrote about conditions, injuries or medications, word for word;
  • your list of injuries and rehab, including any rehab or physio programme you added, word for word, with its end point and whether it is active or resolved. A programme you add from a PDF, photo or camera scan is read on your phone and has identifying details such as your name and contact details removed before it leaves your phone, but it can still name your physio or clinic;
  • your goals (including any nutrition targets, with your answers about your normal day outside workouts and what you want your eating to support), plan, training history and logs, and your notes, check-ins and pain entries;
  • recent Apple Health numbers (daily values for up to the last 28 days), such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, workouts, nutrition and, if you track it, your cycle, plus the scores and trends Red Wellness works out from them;
  • your chat messages;
  • the short list of things Red remembers about you (see What the AI remembers);
  • Red's own earlier plans and briefs, so its advice stays consistent;
  • for the monthly report, the text of lab results or scans you add, with identifying details removed on your phone first;
  • if you train together with someone, the goal's name, its owner's display name and, if you follow the owner's weekly pattern, the kinds of sessions on the owner's days.

What isn't sent:

  • Pictures. When you scan a barcode, a label, a document or a rehab programme, the image is read on your phone and never leaves it.
  • Your voice. If you log by voice, your speech is turned into text on your phone. The audio is never saved and never leaves your phone. Only the text, after you've seen it, is sent.
  • More than the job needs. Food logging sends only the text of what you ate. Saying or typing a recipe's ingredient list sends only that list.

What Anthropic does with it

  • It doesn't train its AI on your data. Its contract with Red Wellness forbids it.
  • It normally deletes it within 30 days. That covers both what Red Wellness sends and Claude's reply.
  • The exception is flagged content. If Anthropic's automatic safety checks flag a request as possibly breaking its rules, it can keep that request for up to 2 years (and the related safety scores for up to 7 years).
  • It works for Red Wellness under a data processing agreement, and only to provide the service.

What the AI remembers

Claude itself remembers nothing between requests. It starts fresh every time, and Red Wellness sends what's needed with each request. (Anthropic keeps a short cache for about 5 minutes so repeated requests are faster.)

The memory lives in your Red Wellness account in Sydney. Along with your plans and logs, Red keeps a short list of things you've told it in chat so it can coach you better, like "prefers evening sessions" or "walks the dog in the mornings". It holds up to 30 short items. Red picks them from your messages, skips passing comments and anything already logged elsewhere (such as pain, injury, illness, medical conditions, medication, goals, training and food), and tidies the list once a week, merging repeats and dropping things whose time has passed. The list is sent to Claude with each plan, brief, report and chat reply.

You can see the list and delete any item, or all of it, in Settings, What Red remembers. Deleting your account deletes it too.

Sending your data overseas

Anthropic stores what Red Wellness sends in the United States, and may process a request in other countries where it runs its AI. Red Wellness can't tell you in advance which country that will be.

US privacy law may not protect your data the way New Zealand's does, and the same goes for privacy law in any other country where a request is processed. So the protection your data gets at Anthropic comes mainly from Red Wellness's contract with Anthropic, rather than from the law there. When Red Wellness asks whether you're happy for it to use AI, choosing "Sounds good" means you agree to this.

Who at Red Wellness sees your data

No one at Red Wellness looks at your data as a matter of course. Ruby, who runs Red Wellness, can access the database only to keep the service running or fix a problem, and reads a report only if someone reports you. If you use Report issue on one of Red's replies, Ruby reads that reply, your message before it and your note, so she can fix what went wrong. A report is not sent to Anthropic.

Withdrawing (turning AI off)

You can withdraw your permission anytime. Red Wellness can't work without AI, so withdrawing means deleting your Red Wellness account. In Settings, go to AI and tap Turn off AI, then confirm. Red Wellness stops sending anything to Anthropic and deletes your account and everything in it. If you cancel, nothing changes. Anything Anthropic already has is normally deleted within 30 days, or kept for up to 2 years if it was flagged by a safety check (related safety scores for up to 7 years).
