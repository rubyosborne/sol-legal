SOL legal

Last updated: 2026-10-26

SOL is a personal health and training companion app for iPhone, built and run by Ruby Osborne in Auckland, New Zealand. It uses your own training logs, check-ins and the Apple Health data you choose to share to write you a daily brief, a weekly plan and replies in chat. SOL supports general wellness and training. It is not a medical device, and it cannot diagnose, treat or prescribe. The two documents below set out the rules for using SOL and exactly what happens to your data.
