1. What Red Wellness is

Red Wellness is a personal health and training companion for iPhone. It takes the information you give it (your onboarding answers, your training logs, your check-ins and your chat messages) together with the Apple Health data you choose to share. The app's AI coach, Red, uses that to generate a daily brief, a weekly training plan and conversational replies.

Red Wellness is a general wellness and fitness product. It is not a medical device, and it is not registered or certified as one. Red Wellness does not practise medicine, nursing, dietetics, physiotherapy or any other regulated profession.

It is not assessed or prescribed by a physiotherapist or any other health professional, and it is not treatment for an injury. Stop it and see a physio if it hurts, if the pain is getting worse, or if you are not sure it is right for you.

3.Red Wellness's output is generated by AI, and it can be wrong

Red Wellness's briefs, weekly plans and chat replies are generated by large language models (currently Anthropic's Claude models, accessed through the Anthropic API). You are reading machine-generated text.

That has consequences you should hold onto:

  • It can be confidently wrong. AI-generated text can contain mistakes, invented details, misread numbers and bad reasoning, and it can be wrong in a tone that sounds certain.
  • It does not know everything about you. Red Wellness only sees the data it has been given. It does not know your medical history, your medications, your injuries or how you actually feel today unless you tell it.
  • It is not consistent. The same question can produce different answers, and a plan may change between weeks.
  • Numbers can be off. Metrics from Apple Health, wearables and your own logs are estimates. Red Wellness's interpretation of them is a further estimate.

You stay responsible for your own training decisions. Red Wellness suggests; you decide. Use your own judgement, listen to your body, and stop if something hurts. If a session, load, intensity or dietary suggestion from Red Wellness does not seem right or safe for you, do not do it. We do not accept responsibility for the consequences of following AI-generated suggestions, to the extent the law allows us to say that (see section 9).
